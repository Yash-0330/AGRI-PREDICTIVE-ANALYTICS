
# AGRICULTURAL BUSINESS INSIGHTS
## Vidarbha Crop Yield Prediction Project

### 1. Business Problem

Agricultural stakeholders need reliable information about expected crop yield to support procurement planning, resource allocation and crop performance monitoring.

This project explores how machine learning can support district-level crop yield estimation.

### 2. Business Objectives

- Estimate crop yield in quintals per hectare.
- Compare yield patterns across six Vidarbha districts.
- Understand the influence of rainfall and soil-related features.
- Identify crops and districts with higher prediction errors.
- Explore data-driven procurement planning.

### 3. Potential Business Applications

**Procurement Planning**

Predicted yield multiplied by cultivated area can provide an initial production estimate. Actual procurement planning should include uncertainty and independent production verification.

**Agricultural Risk Monitoring**

Rainfall-related features can help identify records where weather conditions strongly influence model predictions.

**Crop Performance Analysis**

Comparing predicted yields across districts and crops can support further investigation into agricultural performance.

**Resource Planning**

Soil and rainfall patterns can inform further analysis of agricultural resource requirements.

### 5. Recommendations

1. Validate predictions against real agricultural records.
2. Investigate districts and crops with high prediction errors.
3. Monitor rainfall and soil-related conditions.
4. Use prediction ranges and uncertainty estimates for planning.
5. Retrain and evaluate the model when reliable new data becomes available.

### 6. Conclusion

The project demonstrates how agricultural data analytics and machine learning can be combined to build a prototype crop yield prediction system.

The current results are based on synthetic data and should be treated as a technical demonstration rather than verified agricultural business forecasts.
